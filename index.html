    Rules

    How to play

    1. Bat beats Ball by scoring the delivery.
    2. Ball beats Stump by breaking the wicket.
    3. Stump beats Bat through a dismissal.
    4. Matching choices are a tie and award no point.
    5. The first player to the selected target wins.

    Adaptive mode studies your earlier choices within the current match. It never sends or stores personal data.
